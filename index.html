Use it like an app

Add Sky Lane Weather to your home screen — it gets its own icon and opens full-screen, no app store needed.

🍎 iPhone / iPad

  1. Open this site in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add (top right) — the 🚴 icon appears on your home screen

🤖 Android

  1. Open this site in Chrome
  2. If an Install app banner pops up, just tap it — done
  3. Otherwise tap the ⋮ menu (top right) and choose Add to Home screen / Install app
  4. Confirm — the 🚴 icon appears on your home screen

From the home screen it launches instantly, full-screen, like any other app.
